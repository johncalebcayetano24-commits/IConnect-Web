ABOUT US · WELCOME TO THE FAMILY

Faith brings us together.
Love moves us forward.

IConnect Ministry is a Christ-centered community in Cabuyao City, Laguna, that helps people connect with God and with one another.

We believe every person is valuable and called to influence others positively. Through worship, the Word, fellowship, and service, we aim to see lives and communities transformed by the love of Jesus Christ.

Privacy policy

This notice covers the IConnect Ministry informational website serving visitors in the Philippines. It does not cover all ministry activities, event records, or services operated by other organizations.

Who to contact

For a website privacy question, contact IConnect Ministry through its existing Facebook page (opens in a new tab) and identify your message as a website privacy request. Information involved in a visit

The provider, applicable processing basis, and log-retention settings have not yet been verified.

Choices, purposes, and retention

The saved theme is used only to remember your display choice. It remains until you use the reset control or remove the site’s browser data. You can browse using your device theme without saving a choice.

Your choice to load Google Maps applies only while the location popup remains open. “Remove Google map” or closing the popup removes the embed and stops further requests from that embed. It cannot erase data already received by Google or cookies that Google has already set. Review Google’s Privacy Policy (opens in a new tab) for its processing, international transfers, retention, and controls.

Messages you choose to send through Facebook are handled outside this page. Meta and the ministry may process information you provide there. Contact the ministry for information about its handling and retention of those messages. This notice does not assert a retention period for records we have not verified.
